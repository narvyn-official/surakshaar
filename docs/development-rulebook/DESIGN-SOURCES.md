## Coverage and limitations

The 21st.dev category inventory covers the 29 Marketing Blocks and 49 UI Components labels visible in its public directory. Separators and extended workflow/art families are added independently. No changing item counts are asserted. The other sites are a curated selection, not the complete internet.

React Bits was inspected through its official repository. Origin UI redirected to coss ui. Awwwards, Refero, Motion Primitives, Rive accessibility, the initial Carbon URL, and Three.js responsive documentation were unavailable or insufficiently readable in this pass; they are not counted as inspected sources. The current Carbon motion page was subsequently located and read. Paid individual Mobbin flows were not reviewed.

The main handbook retains its earlier sources. The 34 entries here include overlapping source URLs, so do not add the two source counts and call the result unique websites.
