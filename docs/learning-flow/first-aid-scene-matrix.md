# First-aid teaching scenes — presentation version 1

Implemented 2026-10-10. This is a local presentation enhancement for the eight existing adult first-aid scenarios and the legacy twelve-stage bay. It does not introduce new medical scenarios, change answers, alter scenario fingerprints, grant a certificate, or claim physical first-aid competence.

## Architecture and controls

`FirstAidSceneGuide` is the single map from the immutable `(scenarioId, stepId)` to visible prop groups, short English/Hindi captions and allowed illustration motion. `FirstAidTeachingProps` provides original offline geometry. `FirstAidBayActivity` supplies the same key to `AnatomyScene` in AR and ordinary 3D. A successful action leaves its illustration visible during feedback, then Next shows the following stage.

The apparatus uses the placed body's existing mat coordinate system and scale. The body can roll within that frame; apparatus stays upright on its mat. Apparatus rendering and picking use the same inverse transform. There is no second anchor and Freeze/Replay never changes placement. Existing body markers, held-pressure input, named alternatives, role checks, tracking checks, attempt journaling and saved-record validation remain authoritative.

Freeze/Replay are 48 dp native icon controls over the viewport only for animated demonstrations, with accessible names and tooltips. A separate detail-focus control frames the current body region and apparatus in ordinary 3D; AR keeps its camera and anchor fixed. Whole-body fit remains available in View. Ordinary practice Pause still saves the attempt and stops the scene. Motion respects Android's animation-disabled setting, is paused in the background and has a bounded frame cadence. Static stages require no continuous non-AR rendering. Meshes are built once and reused; existing renderer/context disposal handles their GL buffers.

The step text is concise. Cue retains the complete original scenario condition and citation. None of the app's public-source update results enters these lessons automatically.

## Direct actions and accessible alternatives

The tap control, attached sleeve, loose cover, side dressings around the embedded object, leg supports, head cushion and cooling fan can be selected directly in the relevant stage. These are aliases to existing correct decision IDs, not new or privileged actions. They pass through the same paused/finished/role/tracking checks. The ordinary text action choices remain available; body-location inspection and held-pressure input retain their original markers and 3D alternatives. A direct action never carries across to a different stage.

## Source checks

Primary guidance inspected on 2026-10-10:

- [NHS: Burns and scalds](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/burns-and-scalds/) — cool running water for 20 minutes, leave adhered clothing, loose covering without circumferential wrapping, escalation. The looping water is not a 20-minute treatment timer.
- [St John Ambulance: Severe bleeding](https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/severe-bleeding/) — barrier protection, dressing/direct pressure, and pressure around an embedded object without removal.
- [St John Ambulance: Recovery position](https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/recovery-position/) — normal-breathing unresponsive adult, supporting bent knee and hand at cheek, airway and continuing observation. The distinct small pose guide is schematic; the anatomical source mesh is unrigged.
- [St John Ambulance: Fractures](https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/fractures-and-broken-bones/) — support in the found position, padding, avoiding unnecessary movement, concerning changes in sensation.
- [NHS: Seizure first aid](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/what-to-do-if-someone-has-a-seizure-fit/) — cushion head, remove nearby dangers, do not restrain or put anything in the mouth, note time, reassess after movements stop.
- [NHS: Fainting](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/fainting/) and the scenario's [St John Ambulance fainting source](https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/fainting/) — response/breathing checks and appropriate positioning for the existing scenario; not a universal positioning instruction for trauma or pregnancy.
- [NHS: Heat exhaustion and heatstroke](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heat-exhaustion-heatstroke/) — cool environment, skin cooling and fanning, drinking only when appropriate, urgent response to confusion/deterioration.
- [Resuscitation Council UK: Adult BLS 2025](https://www.resus.org.uk/professional-library/2025-resuscitation-guidelines/adult-basic-life-support-guidelines) — early help, abnormal gasps, chest-centre hand position, AED instructions and staying clear. The illustrative hand loop is authored around 110 cycles/minute but is **not** a rate/depth/force trainer or metronome. Device timing and screen scale cannot establish real CPR performance.

The cited pages are checked source guidance, not approval by their publishers. Qualified clinical review and Hindi language review remain pending; no professional review is represented by this work. Body movement, airway opening, physiological breathing, pressure, cooling, bleeding control and patient outcomes are not physically measured.
