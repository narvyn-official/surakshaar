# SurakshaAR

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

Learners in schools, colleges, workplaces and training institutions. Authorised trainers and administrators manage institutional learning and staff reference material.

## Product Purpose

Teach fire safety and first aid through guided lessons, interactive spatial illustrations and practice. Keep learning available offline and preserve separate assessment and certification workflows.

## Operating Context

Android 10+ phones, handheld AR, on-screen 3D, and a Cardboard-style viewer with a button and an uncovered rear camera. Camera passthrough maps one rear-camera image into each calibrated lens; it does not provide stereo camera depth. Physical phone and viewer verification is pending for the current build.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The repository contains native Kotlin/OpenGL learning activities, ARCore tracking, MediaPipe hand landmarks and a calibrated Cardboard camera compositor. Separate VR and virtual-room modes are retired; historical decision records remain readable. Authored lesson content and role/workspace rules must remain intact. Gesture exploration does not establish clinical competence. Hindi and English are available; Santali review remains pending.

## Brand Commitments

SurakshaAR. Light, minimal native screens, concise labels, semantic action colours, direct controls and proper Android Back behavior. The user expressly requests neumorphism across the app. No four-point sparkle decorations or northeast arrow icons.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve native control semantics, visible focus, sufficient contrast, readable large text and alternatives to camera gestures. Routine screens should fit the viewport; longer required information uses accessible paged content rather than hidden essential controls.

## Evidence on Hand

Real authored catalogues under content/, licensed anatomy, procedural equipment, Android unit/instrumentation tests and historical device reports. Current emulator evidence does not substitute for live hand, phone or headset verification.
