# SurakshaAR tactile interface

The user-pinned direction is a light, minimal neumorphic native app. Use a cool neutral canvas, original soft offset shadow surfaces and deep blue-teal actions. Shadows express elevation; inset states express selection or press. Text, focus and actionable boundaries remain explicit, including when shadows are not perceived.

Home is the next learning action; Learn is the course catalogue; Explore is model and viewing tools; Progress is the record. Avoid duplicating the full course catalogue on Home. Inside spatial learning, the model occupies the centre, the current step sits in one compact instruction area and controls remain fixed at the edge.

Use the system sans family for native readability and Hindi fallback. Keep semantic fire, warning, danger and success colours restrained. Preserve native Android controls and Back behavior. Do not decorate the camera feed with raised white surfaces that obscure the practice area; the spatial HUD uses the same palette with opaque high-contrast text surfaces.

Implementation is code-first. No external UI, font or raster library is adopted for this redesign. Actual rendered screens and interaction tests govern acceptance.
